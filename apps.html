FormQuest

FreeChrome & Edge

Fills job applications, forms, and timesheets for you, then presses Next and keeps going. Record a form once and replay it any time. No account, no AI usage costs, and your info stays on your computer, with sensitive parts locked by your own password.

Free download
How to install (about a minute)
  1. Unzip the download.
  2. In Chrome or Edge, open the extensions page (type chrome://extensions or edge://extensions in the address bar).
  3. Turn on Developer mode.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped formquest folder.
