Studio tools

Bring characters to life with Lipline.

Two browser tools that turn your voice into talking cartoon characters. Learn them with us, then use them for skits, explainers, voiceover characters, and series content.

Lipline

AnimatorRuns in your browser

A dialogue-driven 2D cartoon animator. Record or upload your lines, pick a character, and Lipline animates it lip-synced to the audio, then exports a video. No manual keyframing.

  • A green-screen background, so scenes can be composited anywhere
  • Saved, reusable characters for series consistency
  • Video export with synced audio

Lipline Live

Live avatarDesktop Microsoft Edge

A talking 2D cartoon avatar that lip-syncs in real time to Claude's voice-mode replies. Build a character, link a voice conversation, and watch the face react as it talks.

  • Build and save characters: skin tone, hair, outfit, and accessories
  • Expressions, gestures, and idle life that follow the voice
  • Green screen and clip recording
  • No API keys needed
