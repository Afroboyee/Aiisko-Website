Services

Work we staff.

Five service lines, each delivered by a crew that knows the job and documents it.

IMACInstall, Move, Add, Change

Hands-on hardware work for workstations and equipment: new deployments, relocations, additions, and swaps.

  • Workstation and peripheral installs
  • Moves between desks and sites
  • Adds, swaps, and break-fix replacements
  • Staging and clean documentation

ITAMIT asset management

Know what you own and where it is. Crews capture the facts on the floor so your records match reality.

  • Physical inventory and audits
  • Serial and barcode capture, tagging
  • Reconciliation against asset records
  • Lifecycle and location updates

E-wasteRetired equipment handling

Retired equipment collected, sorted, and staged with the paperwork to show where it went.

  • Collection and sorting
  • Staging for pickup
  • Chain-of-custody documentation
  • Hand-off to your approved recycler

CleaningData-center and facility

Careful cleaning for technical spaces, done with equipment in mind.

  • Raised-floor and rack-area cleaning
  • Pre- and post-project cleanup
  • Equipment-safe procedures

Warehouse movementReceiving, staging, and moves

Extra hands for the physical side of operations when volume spikes or a project lands.

  • Receiving and put-away
  • Pallet and cage moves
  • Staging and kitting
  • Loading and unloading

How it works

From request to done.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Pick the services, the state, and the timing. A few sentences about the site is plenty.

  2. We line up a crew

    We match the work to contractors in that state and confirm scope, schedule, and terms with you in writing.

  3. The work gets done and documented

    Crews do the job and capture the details, so you have a clear record of what changed.

Questions

Good to know.

Which states do you cover?

We are building crews state by state. Choose your state above and tell us what you need. We will confirm coverage and timing with you directly.

What do I need to join the network?

Experience in at least one service line, a reliable way to get to sites, and a good attitude. Tell us your state, your skills, and your availability, and we will reach out as work opens up near you.

How are the terms of work set?

Scope, schedule, and terms are confirmed in writing with the customer, and with each contractor, before any work begins.

Can you help with something not on the list?

Possibly. Describe the work in the request form and we will tell you plainly whether we can staff it.
